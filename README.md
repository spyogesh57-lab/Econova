# Econova
Understand Economics in a better way , don't just memorize it 
🌍 Econova — Economics, Reimagined

Learn Economics. Understand the World.

Econova is a student-built educational platform designed to make Economics easier to understand, more interactive, and more engaging for Tamil Nadu State Board Class 11 and Class 12 students.

Instead of simply memorizing textbook definitions, students can explore economic concepts, understand how they work in real life, visualize relationships through graphs, and strengthen their knowledge through practice.

🔗 Live Website: https://econnova.netlify.app

🎯 Our Mission

«Students should be able to see how Economics works, not merely repeat what the textbook says.»

Econova aims to make quality Economics learning more accessible by combining clear explanations, interactive learning, and practical understanding in one place.

✨ Key Features

- 📚 Structured Learning — Explore Economics concepts aligned with the Tamil Nadu State Board syllabus.
- 📊 Economic Lab — Discover economic relationships through interactive graphs and visualizations.
- 🧠 AI Tutor — An AI-powered learning experience planned to help students understand concepts.
- 📖 Definitions & Key Terms — Review important economic terminology.
- 🎯 Practice & Revision — Strengthen understanding through questions and revision activities.
- 🎓 Student-Focused Design — A learning experience designed to be simple, accessible, and engaging.

Some features may be under development or planned for future releases.

🔄 Our Learning Approach

Learn → Understand → Visualize → Experiment → Practice → Master

Every step is designed to help students move beyond memorization towards genuine understanding.
